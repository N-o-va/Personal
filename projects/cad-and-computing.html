SolidWorks: parts and a full drawing

The headline piece is the support bracket above. I modelled it from a supplied sketch, using a reference plane to build the triangular rib, extruded in both directions. Then I produced a full drawing: top, front, right and isometric views with complete dimensions, hidden lines, centrelines, and a title block. It's the kind of drawing someone could make the part from.

AutoCAD and Inventor: drafting to standard

In the first semester I drew three profile parts to scale in AutoCAD 2D: a notched plate, a wrench profile, and a lobed flange. I then produced orthographic projections (front, top and right views) of two isometric parts, using separate layers for hidden, centre, dimension, construction, and text lines, and dimensioned them so they could be machined.

I also modelled one of those parts in 3D in Autodesk Inventor and generated its drawing views from the model. Inventor became my main tool for the glove dispenser, which has 28 Inventor part files.

MATLAB: finding maximum power transfer

One task was to sweep a transformer's turns ratio n from 0.01 to 2 and find where the power delivered, Ps, peaks, for Vs = 7 V, Rs = 5 Ω, and R0 = 10 Ω:

P_s = R_s * (n * V_s / (n^2 * R_0 + R_s))^2

My loop found the maximum, 1.225 W at n = 0.71. That matches the analytical result for maximum power transfer, n = √(Rs/R0) ≈ 0.707. Seeing the numerical sweep land on the formula is a good check that both are right. Hover over the curve to read values:

Python: problem solving and working with AI

  • Physics with validation. A program for two masses on an incline over a pulley. It checks that the angle is within 30–45°, computes the threshold mass, checks that the user's mass exceeds it, and reports the acceleration.
  • AI-assisted, human-reviewed. For a file-processing task where AI generation was required, I had Claude write a program that matches maths and chemistry grades across two files. Then I reviewed it critically: I caught that it never used the specified filenames and rewrote the chemistry-reading section by hand. The written report on that review was my own.
