Nexus: a talent platform for the quantum industry

A prototype two-sided hiring platform that connects people looking for work in quantum computing with the companies hiring them. It has separate entry paths for candidates and employers, a six-step onboarding flow, an admin review queue, and a candidate dashboard.

The idea

Quantum computing is a small, specialised field. Candidates and employers both struggle to find each other on general job boards. Nexus is a focused pipeline: candidates describe their quantum focus area (computing, software, sensing, communications, cryptography, materials), and employers browse and review them.

Key flows

1. Onboarding in six steps

Personal info, education, technical skills, work experience, quantum focus, and career goals, each on its own step, with a progress bar and short encouraging copy so a long profile doesn't feel like a wall of fields.

2. Admin review queue

Every application goes through a review queue that is searchable and filterable by status and by type (candidate or employer), with one-click approve and deny.

3. Candidate dashboard

Once approved, a candidate sees their live profile, open roles tagged by focus area, and a preview of matching.

Design decisions

  • Two front doors. "I'm looking for work" and "I'm hiring" split the audience on the first screen, so each side only sees what's relevant to it.
  • Curated, not open. An approval step keeps the network credible, which matters more in a small field than raw sign-up numbers.
  • Be honest about the cold start. Automated matching only works with enough people on both sides. Instead of showing empty or fake matches, the dashboard shows that matching is coming "when we reach scale".

Takeaways

Using an AI app builder moved the hard part from writing code to deciding what the product should do: which screens exist, what each one asks for, and in what order. It was a fast way to test a product idea end to end.
